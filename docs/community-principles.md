# Community Principles

Open Food Systems is built by people — diverse in background, united in purpose.  
These principles guide how we treat one another as we learn, build, and imagine together.

## 1. Dignity First
Every person deserves respect.  
We speak and act in ways that honor the humanity of others — especially those most affected by hunger.

## 2. Curiosity Over Judgment
We ask questions before assuming.  
We listen before responding.  
We welcome perspectives that challenge our own.

## 3. Openness as a Practice
Knowledge grows when shared.  
We document our work, invite contributions, and keep our processes transparent.

## 4. Compassion in Action
We move from intention to care.  
We support one another, uplift communities, and act with empathy — not charity, but solidarity.

## 5. Humility in Learning
No one has all the answers.  
We acknowledge what we don’t know, learn from mistakes, and grow together.

## 6. Collaboration Over Competition
We build bridges, not silos.  
We celebrate collective progress more than individual achievement.

## 7. Responsibility to the Whole
Our actions shape the movement.  
We contribute thoughtfully, communicate clearly, and care for the spaces we share.

## 8. Diversity as Strength
Different experiences, cultures, and voices make us wiser.  
We welcome contributions from anyone who shares the mission.

## 9. Kindness as a Standard
We choose patience.  
We choose generosity.  
We choose to treat one another with care, even when we disagree.

## 10. A Living Culture
These principles evolve as we do.  
They grow with the movement, shaped by the people who carry it forward.
