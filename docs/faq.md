# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## What is Open Food Systems?
It is a global, open, collaborative movement exploring how humanity, AI, and the internet can work together to eliminate hunger.

## Who runs this movement?
No single person or organization runs it.  
It is a collective effort shaped by all of us — people, communities, and the tools we create.

## Is this a company or nonprofit?
Neither.  
It is an open movement, not a formal institution.

## How can I participate?
Anyone can contribute ideas, knowledge, tools, research, or community experience.  
Participation is open, voluntary, and self-directed.

## Do I need technical skills?
Not at all.  
If we care about dignity, fairness, and food access, we already have something valuable to offer.

## Why combine humanity, AI, and the internet?
Because together they form a powerful collective intelligence — one that can help us understand and improve food systems in new ways.

## What is the long-term goal?
A world where hunger is not a reality for anyone, anywhere.

## Is this project political or religious?
It is human.  
It welcomes people of all backgrounds, beliefs, and cultures who share the hope of a world without hunger.

## How can I stay updated?
By visiting this site, contributing ideas, or following the movement as it grows openly and transparently.

Open Food Systems exists because **we** choose to build it together.
