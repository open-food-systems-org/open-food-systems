# Global Goals

These goals represent the shared direction of Open Food Systems.  
They are broad, long-term objectives that guide our collective work toward a world without hunger.

## 1. End Hunger
Ensure that every person, everywhere, has reliable access to nutritious food.  
Hunger is not inevitable — it is solvable through collaboration, knowledge, and community leadership.

## 2. Strengthen Local Food Systems
Support communities in building food systems that are resilient, fair, and rooted in local knowledge and culture.

## 3. Open Knowledge for All
Make research, data, tools, and insights freely accessible so anyone can learn, contribute, and innovate.

## 4. Empower Communities
Shift power toward people and communities, enabling them to shape their own food futures with dignity and autonomy.

## 5. Build Sustainable and Regenerative Practices
Encourage food systems that protect ecosystems, restore soil, conserve water, and support long-term planetary health.

## 6. Foster Global Collaboration
Connect individuals, communities, researchers, technologists, and organizations across borders to share ideas and solutions.

## 7. Use Technology Responsibly
Ensure that AI, data, and digital tools serve humanity — supporting equity, transparency, and human dignity.

## 8. Inspire a Culture of Care
Promote compassion, solidarity, and shared responsibility as core values in how we feed one another.

These goals are not endpoints.  
They are commitments — a shared direction for all of us working toward a more just and nourished world.
