# Mission

The mission of Open Food Systems is simple and ambitious:  
to explore how open knowledge, open technology, and global collaboration can help eliminate hunger.

## What we aim to do
- Understand the systems that shape food access  
- Identify opportunities for open-source solutions  
- Support community-led food initiatives  
- Share knowledge freely and transparently  
- Build tools that empower, not replace, people  

## What guides us
- Dignity for every human being  
- Equity in access to food and resources  
- Collaboration across borders and cultures  
- Openness in knowledge, tools, and methods  
- A belief that hunger is solvable  

## Why this matters
Food is not just a resource — it is a human right.  
A world without hunger is possible when people choose to build it together.
