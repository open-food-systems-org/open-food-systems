# Get Involved

Open Food Systems is a movement built by all of us — people, communities, and the tools we create together.  
There is no membership, no barrier, no permission needed.  
If we care about dignity, fairness, and a world without hunger, we are already part of it.

## Ways to get involved
- Share ideas or insights  
- Contribute research or knowledge  
- Build open-source tools  
- Support local food initiatives  
- Connect with others working on similar challenges  
- Help shape the direction of the movement  

## Everyone has something to offer
We don’t need to be experts.  
We don’t need to be perfect.  
We simply need to care — and to act.

## Start where you are
Whether we are individuals, communities, technologists, farmers, students, or simply people who want a better world, we can contribute in our own way.

The movement grows because **we** choose to grow it together.
