# Movement Framework

Open Food Systems is a movement built on dignity, care, and shared humanity.  
This framework shows how the movement’s elements fit together —  
not as a hierarchy, but as a living, interconnected whole.

It is a map of the movement’s architecture.

## Core Principles
The foundation of everything:
- **Dignity**  
- **Care**  
- **Humanity**  
- **Openness**  
- **Humility**

These principles guide every action, every Circle, every story, every practice.

## Pillars of the Movement
The movement rests on several interconnected pillars:

### Community Circles  
Local gatherings where people share food, care, and connection.

### Movement Practices  
Simple, repeatable rhythms that weave the movement into daily life.

### Movement Language  
A shared way of speaking that honors dignity and deepens connection.

### Movement Symbols  
Visual and embodied expressions of the movement’s spirit.

### Stories of Care  
Human moments that reflect the heart of the movement.

### Pathways of Participation  
Gentle routes for people to walk, based on their interests and energy.

### Movement Evolution  
A living philosophy of how the movement grows, adapts, and renews itself.

Each pillar supports the others.  
Each one strengthens the whole.

## How the Pieces Interconnect
The movement is not linear.  
It is a web of relationships.

- **Circles** use the **language** and **symbols**.  
- **Practices** generate **stories**.  
- **Stories** inspire new **circles**.  
- **Pathways** guide participation within all pillars.  
- **Evolution** ensures everything stays alive and grounded.  
- **Principles** anchor every part of the movement.

Nothing stands alone.  
Everything is connected.

## The Movement Flow
A simple cycle reflects how the movement lives:

**Care → Connection → Community → Creation → Reflection → Renewal → Care**

This flow repeats at every scale —  
in a single meal, a Circle gathering, a season of activity, or the movement as a whole.

## Entering the Movement
There is no single entry point.  
People can begin anywhere:

- by sharing a meal  
- by joining a Circle  
- by offering care  
- by learning  
- by creating  
- by telling a story  
- by practicing dignity  

Every entry is valid.  
Every path is welcome.

## A Living Framework
This framework is not fixed.  
It evolves as the movement grows.

Its purpose is simple:
to help people see the whole,  
understand the connections,  
and walk with clarity and intention.
