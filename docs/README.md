# Open Food Systems
An open-source humanitarian initiative to explore AI-driven food systems for global equity.

## Mission
Open Food Systems exists to explore and develop technologies that can help eliminate hunger through open, transparent, and collaborative innovation.

## Dedication
This project is dedicated to President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, whose lifelong commitment to ending hunger continues to inspire global action.

## What We Are Building
- AI-driven nutrition models
- Open hardware for food printing
- Sustainable ingredient logistics
- A global learning platform
- The Food OS concept device

See the /docs, /hardware, /software, /logistics, /community, and /course directories for detailed materials.
