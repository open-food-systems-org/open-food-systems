# Contact

Open Food Systems is an open, collaborative movement.  
There is no central office or formal organization — just people, communities, and tools working together toward a world without hunger.

## How to reach us
Because this movement is open and distributed, the best way to connect is through:

- Discussions on the project’s public pages  
- Contributions to ideas, tools, or research  
- Community conversations as they emerge  
- Sharing insights, questions, or proposals openly  

## Why we use open communication
We believe in transparency.  
We believe in collective intelligence.  
We believe that ideas grow stronger when shared in the open.

## A living, evolving movement
As the movement grows, new ways to connect will emerge — community spaces, forums, shared tools, and more.

For now, the simplest way to reach us is to participate openly.  
Every voice matters, and every contribution helps shape what we are building together.
