# Principles

These principles guide the work of Open Food Systems. They are not rules, but commitments — a way of working that keeps the movement grounded, honest, and human-centered.

## 1. Openness
Knowledge, tools, and methods should be accessible to everyone.  
Transparency is a strength, not a risk.

## 2. Dignity
Every human being deserves respect, nourishment, and agency.  
No solution is acceptable if it undermines human dignity.

## 3. Collaboration
Food systems are global and interconnected.  
Solutions must be built with communities, not imposed on them.

## 4. Equity
Fairness is not optional.  
We work to reduce disparities, not reinforce them.

## 5. Sustainability
Food systems must support life — human life, ecological life, and future life.  
Short-term gains cannot justify long-term harm.

## 6. Community Leadership
Communities know their needs better than any institution.  
We listen first, act second.

## 7. Responsibility
Technology is powerful.  
We use it with care, humility, and accountability.

These principles will evolve as the movement grows, but their spirit will remain constant.
