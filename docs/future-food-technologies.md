# Future Food Technologies

Future Food Technologies describes the full technological and industrial ecosystem that may support the next evolution of global food systems.

This ecosystem extends far beyond food production itself. It includes manufacturing, infrastructure, sanitation, legal frameworks, logistics, education, and many other sectors that ensure food systems operate safely and reliably.

Open Food Systems assumes that any large-scale transformation in how food is produced and distributed will involve the coordinated participation of many industries.

The purpose of this page is to map the technological landscape that may support such a transformation.
