# Community

Open Food Systems is a collective effort. Anyone who believes in dignity, equity, and the possibility of a world without hunger is welcome here.

## How people can participate
- Share knowledge  
- Contribute ideas  
- Build tools  
- Support local food initiatives  
- Connect with others working toward similar goals  
- Help shape the direction of the movement  

## What we value in our community
- Respect  
- Curiosity  
- Collaboration  
- Openness  
- A willingness to learn and grow  

## A movement for everyone
You do not need to be a technologist, a researcher, or an expert.  
If you care about people, food, and fairness, you belong here.

This movement grows through shared effort — humanity, AI, and the internet working together toward a common good.
