# The Movement in One Page

Open Food Systems is a global, open, collaborative movement exploring how humanity, AI, and the internet can work together to eliminate hunger.

## What we believe
- Hunger is not inevitable.  
- Dignity is universal.  
- Knowledge should be open.  
- Communities know themselves best.  
- Technology must serve humanity.  
- Collaboration is more powerful than competition.  

## What we do
- Share knowledge openly  
- Build tools that help communities  
- Map and understand food systems  
- Support local, community-led solutions  
- Connect people, ideas, and resources globally  

## How we work
- In the open  
- With transparency  
- Through collective intelligence  
- With humility and compassion  
- As a living, evolving movement  

## Our long-term direction
A world where:
- every person has access to nutritious food  
- communities have control over their food systems  
- knowledge and tools are open to all  
- technology uplifts humanity  
- dignity is the foundation of every system  

## Who we are
People from everywhere — individuals, communities, researchers, technologists, farmers, students, and anyone who believes hunger should not exist.

Open Food Systems belongs to all of us.  
This is our shared beginning.
