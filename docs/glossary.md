# Glossary

This glossary explains key terms used throughout Open Food Systems.  
It is a living document that will grow as our movement evolves.

## Food Systems
The networks of people, resources, and processes involved in producing, distributing, and consuming food.

## Food Sovereignty
The right of people and communities to define their own food systems and have control over how food is produced and accessed.

## Food Security
Reliable access to sufficient, safe, and nutritious food for a healthy life.

## Open Knowledge
Information that is freely accessible, shareable, and reusable by anyone.

## Open Source
Tools, software, or methods whose underlying code or structure is publicly available for anyone to use, modify, or improve.

## Collective Intelligence
The combined knowledge, insight, and creativity of many people — and the tools they use — working together.

## Collaboration
Working together across borders, cultures, and disciplines to solve shared challenges.

## Sustainability
Practices that support long-term ecological, social, and economic health.

## Equity
Fairness in access to resources, opportunities, and outcomes.

## Movement
A shared effort driven by people, ideas, and purpose — not by hierarchy or institutions.

This glossary exists to help all of us speak a common language as we build this movement together.
