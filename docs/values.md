# Values

These values reflect the heart of Open Food Systems.  
They guide our intentions, our actions, and the way we relate to one another as we work toward a world without hunger.

## Humanity
People come first.  
Every decision, tool, and idea must honor human dignity and well‑being.

## Compassion
We act with empathy.  
We recognize the struggles of others and respond with care, not judgment.

## Openness
Knowledge grows when it is shared.  
We choose transparency, accessibility, and collaboration over secrecy or control.

## Humility
We do not have all the answers.  
We listen, learn, and adapt as we grow together.

## Stewardship
We treat food, land, technology, and knowledge as shared responsibilities — not possessions.

## Solidarity
We stand with communities, not above them.  
We work alongside those who face hunger, inequality, or exclusion.

## Hope
We believe a better world is possible.  
Hope is not naïve — it is a commitment to action.

These values are not fixed.  
They evolve as **we** evolve, as our movement grows, and as we learn from one another.
