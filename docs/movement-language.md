# Movement Language

Open Food Systems is a movement rooted in dignity, care, and shared humanity.  
The language we use reflects these values.  
It shapes how we relate to one another and how we show up in the world.

This is not a rulebook.  
It is a guide — a way of speaking that honors the people we serve and the communities we build.

## The Tone of the Movement
Our tone is:
- warm  
- humble  
- clear  
- human  
- grounded  

We speak in ways that invite, not impose.  
We choose words that create connection, not distance.

## Key Phrases of the Movement
These phrases carry the spirit of Open Food Systems.  
They can open a Circle, begin a meal, or accompany an act of care.

- **“There is always room at the table.”**  
- **“We begin with dignity.”**  
- **“Care is shared.”**  
- **“Food connects us.”**  
- **“We grow together.”**  
- **“Everyone belongs.”**  

They are simple, universal, and meant to be spoken with sincerity.

## Words We Choose
We choose language that:
- respects dignity  
- centers humanity  
- avoids labels  
- acknowledges agency  
- reflects humility  

Instead of “the needy,” we say:
- **people in our community**  
- **people facing hunger**  
- **people experiencing hardship**  

Instead of “helping the poor,” we say:
- **sharing care**  
- **supporting one another**  
- **standing in solidarity**  

Language shapes how we see people — and how they see themselves.

## Words We Avoid
Not as prohibitions, but as reminders.

We avoid words that:
- reduce people to their circumstances  
- imply hierarchy  
- create distance  
- frame care as charity  

Words like:
- “beneficiaries”  
- “recipients”  
- “the needy”  
- “the less fortunate”  

These terms unintentionally diminish dignity.  
We choose language that lifts people up.

## How We Tell Stories
When we share stories of care:
- we center the humanity of the person receiving  
- we avoid making ourselves the hero  
- we speak with humility  
- we protect privacy and dignity  
- we focus on connection, not credit  

A story is not a performance.  
It is a reflection of shared humanity.

## Speaking in Circles
When Circles gather, language helps create a sense of belonging.

A Circle may begin with:
- **“We gather with care.”**  
- **“Everyone is welcome.”**  
- **“We listen with openness.”**  

And close with:
- **“May our care ripple outward.”**  
- **“We leave with gratitude.”**  
- **“Until we gather again.”**  

These phrases are invitations to presence.

## A Living Language
Movement language evolves as the movement grows.  
It adapts to cultures, communities, and contexts.

What matters most is this:
**We speak in ways that honor dignity, deepen connection, and reflect our shared humanity.**
