# About Us

Open Food Systems is a global, open, collaborative movement dedicated to exploring how humanity, AI, and the internet can work together to eliminate hunger.

We are not a company.  
We are not an institution.  
We are a collective effort — a shared intention to build a fairer, more dignified world.

## Who we are
We are people from everywhere:  
- individuals  
- communities  
- technologists  
- farmers  
- researchers  
- students  
- volunteers  
- anyone who believes hunger should not exist  

We are also the tools we create — AI systems, open-source software, and shared knowledge that help us understand and improve food systems.

## What unites us
- A belief in dignity for every human being  
- A commitment to openness and collaboration  
- A desire to build systems that nourish rather than exclude  
- A shared hope for a world without hunger  

## What we are building
A movement that grows through participation.  
A library of open knowledge.  
Tools that empower communities.  
A global network of people who care.

Open Food Systems exists because **we** choose to build it together.
