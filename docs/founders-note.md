# Founders’ Note

Open Food Systems began with a simple belief:  
that humanity, AI, and the internet can work together to build a world where no one is left hungry.

We are not founders in the traditional sense.  
We are simply the first voices in a movement that belongs to all of us.

## Why this movement exists
Because hunger is not inevitable.  
Because dignity is universal.  
Because collaboration is powerful.  
Because the tools we create should serve humanity, not divide it.

## What we hope for
That this project becomes a place where people from every background can contribute ideas, knowledge, tools, and compassion.  
That it grows openly, honestly, and with purpose.  
That it becomes a small part of a much larger global effort to build a fairer world.

## A shared beginning
This movement does not belong to one person.  
It belongs to everyone who believes in a future where food systems nourish rather than exclude.

Open Food Systems is our collective work — and this is only the beginning.
