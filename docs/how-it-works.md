# How It Works

Open Food Systems is not a traditional organization. It is a distributed, open, collaborative movement powered by humanity, AI, and the internet.

## 1. Open Knowledge
Everything we learn, build, or discover is shared openly.  
No paywalls, no gatekeepers, no hidden systems.

## 2. Collective Intelligence
We combine human insight, community experience, and AI tools to explore solutions to hunger and food inequality.

## 3. Distributed Participation
Anyone can contribute from anywhere:
- ideas  
- research  
- tools  
- local knowledge  
- community experience  

There is no central authority.  
The movement grows through shared effort.

## 4. Transparent Process
All pages, discussions, and documents are public.  
We build in the open so others can learn, adapt, and improve on our work.

## 5. Continuous Evolution
This movement is alive.  
As we learn more, we adapt.  
As the world changes, we respond.

Open Food Systems works because **we** work together.
