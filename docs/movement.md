# The Open Food Systems Movement

Open Food Systems is a global, open-source initiative dedicated to exploring how technology, AI, and collective intelligence can help eliminate hunger. This is not a company, not a startup, not a product — it is a movement.

## Why this matters
Hunger is not a technological problem. It is a systems problem.  
We have enough food, enough knowledge, and enough global capacity to feed every human being — yet millions still go hungry.

Open Food Systems exists to explore new models of:
- food production  
- food distribution  
- food logistics  
- food sovereignty  
- community-led innovation  
- open-source collaboration  

## What we believe
- Knowledge should be open.  
- Tools should be accessible.  
- Communities should be empowered.  
- Technology should serve humanity.  
- Hunger should not exist.  

## What comes next

More pages, ideas, and contributions will be added as the project grows.
