# Living Documents

Open Food Systems is a living movement — and our documents reflect that.  
Nothing here is final. Nothing is fixed. Everything evolves as we learn, grow, and collaborate.

## Why our documents are living
Because the world changes.  
Because communities change.  
Because knowledge expands.  
Because new voices join the movement every day.

Static documents belong to institutions.  
Living documents belong to people.

## What this means in practice
- Pages may be updated as new insights emerge  
- Ideas may evolve as communities contribute  
- Language may shift as we grow in understanding  
- Tools and frameworks may improve over time  

This is not a sign of instability — it is a sign of life.

## A movement that adapts
We do not aim for perfection.  
We aim for progress, clarity, and openness.

Every document in this project is an invitation:  
to refine, to question, to contribute, to imagine.

## Built by all of us
These documents are shaped by the collective intelligence of humanity, AI, and the internet.  
They grow because **we** grow.

Open Food Systems is alive — and so are the words that guide it.
