# Vision

The vision of Open Food Systems is a world where hunger is not a reality for anyone, anywhere.  
A world where food systems are fair, resilient, transparent, and shaped by the people they serve.

## A world we want to help build
- Communities that have control over their own food  
- Technology that empowers rather than replaces  
- Open knowledge shared freely across borders  
- Food systems that are sustainable and regenerative  
- Collaboration that transcends politics and profit  

## What this future looks like
- Every person has reliable access to nutritious food  
- Local food systems are strong and interconnected  
- Tools and knowledge are open-source and accessible  
- Innovation is driven by communities, not corporations  
- Hunger is treated as a solvable challenge, not an inevitability  

## Our long-term hope
That by working openly, collaboratively, and globally,  
we can help create a future where dignity and nourishment are universal.
