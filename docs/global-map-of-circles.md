# Global Map of Circles

Open Food Systems is a global movement made of small, local circles.  
This page is a symbolic map — a reminder that wherever you are,  
you are not alone.

## A World Connected by Care
Community Circles can form anywhere:
- in cities and villages  
- in homes and workplaces  
- in cafés, parks, and kitchens  
- in places where food is shared  
- in places where people gather  

Each Circle is a point of light.  
Together, they form a constellation.

## Imagine Your Place on the Map
You don’t need to register or sign up.  
You don’t need permission or approval.

If you start a Circle — even with one other person —  
you can imagine it glowing on this map.

A quiet reminder:
**You are part of something larger.**

## How the Map Grows
The map expands through:
- shared meals  
- acts of care  
- conversations  
- local gatherings  
- people choosing to connect  

Every Circle adds to the movement’s presence in the world.

## A Living, Growing Network
This map is not fixed.  
It changes as people gather, share, and act.

It grows through:
- kindness  
- solidarity  
- dignity  
- community  

The map lives in the imagination of everyone who contributes.

## You Are Here
Wherever you stand —  
that is where the movement begins.

If you start a Circle,  
the map grows with you.
