# Movement Constellation

Open Food Systems is a constellation —  
a living ecosystem of interconnected elements,  
each one reflecting the movement’s spirit of dignity, care, and shared humanity.

The constellation helps us see the whole movement at once,  
not as a structure to follow,  
but as a landscape to inhabit.

## The Core: Ethos and Principles
At the center of the constellation is the movement’s inner light:
- dignity  
- care  
- humanity  
- openness  
- humility  

These principles form the gravitational center.  
Everything orbits around them.

## The Inner Ring: Daily Expressions
Closest to the core are the elements that shape everyday life in the movement:

### Movement Practices  
Simple, repeatable rhythms that bring dignity and care into daily life.

### Movement Language  
A shared way of speaking that honors humanity.

### Movement Symbols  
Visual and embodied expressions of the movement’s spirit.

These elements are the daily touchpoints —  
the ways the movement is lived moment by moment.

## The Middle Ring: Communal Expressions
The next layer holds the elements that bring people together:

### Community Circles  
Spaces of belonging, reflection, and shared meals.

### Stories of Care  
Human moments that illuminate the movement’s heart.

### Movement Invitations  
Open doors that welcome people into the movement.

These elements create connection and community.

## The Outer Ring: Growth and Movement
The outer ring reflects how people move, grow, and shape the movement:

### Pathways of Participation  
Gentle routes for people to walk based on their energy and interests.

### Movement Journey  
A cyclical, human way of moving through the movement.

### Movement Evolution  
How the movement adapts, renews, and stays alive.

These elements describe the movement’s flow over time.

## The Framework: The Connective Tissue
The Movement Framework weaves all elements together.  
It shows how:
- practices support stories  
- stories inspire circles  
- circles use language and symbols  
- pathways guide participation  
- evolution keeps everything alive  
- principles anchor every part  

The framework is not a hierarchy.  
It is the architecture of relationship.

## Seeing the Whole
The constellation reveals a simple truth:

**The movement is not one thing.  
It is many things held together by dignity and care.**

People can enter anywhere.  
They can move freely.  
They can walk at their own pace.

The constellation is a map,  
but the movement is alive.
