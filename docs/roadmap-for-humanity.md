# Roadmap for Humanity

This roadmap is not a schedule.  
It is a horizon — a long‑term vision of what humanity, AI, and the internet can build together when we choose collaboration over competition.

## Phase 1: Understanding (Today–5 years)
- Map global food systems openly  
- Gather knowledge from communities, researchers, and lived experience  
- Build simple tools that help us see the whole picture  
- Share everything freely so others can build on it  

## Phase 2: Empowerment (5–15 years)
- Support community‑led food initiatives with open tools  
- Strengthen local food systems through shared knowledge  
- Create open-source models for resilience, sustainability, and equity  
- Connect communities globally so they can learn from one another  

## Phase 3: Transformation (15–30 years)
- Help shift food systems toward fairness and dignity  
- Encourage policies and practices rooted in openness and collaboration  
- Build global networks of shared intelligence — human and artificial  
- Support regenerative, sustainable, community‑driven food ecosystems  

## Phase 4: A World Without Hunger (30+ years)
A world where:
- every person has reliable access to nutritious food  
- communities have control over their own food systems  
- knowledge and tools are open to all  
- technology serves humanity, not the other way around  

## A movement across generations
This roadmap is not ours alone.  
It belongs to everyone who believes hunger should not exist.

We may not see every milestone in our lifetime —  
but we begin the journey so future generations can continue it.

This is our shared horizon.
