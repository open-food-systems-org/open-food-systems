# Pathways of Participation

Open Food Systems is a movement of many paths.  
There are no roles, ranks, or titles — only ways of walking.

Each person participates in the way that feels natural,  
based on their energy, interests, and context.

These pathways are invitations, not expectations.

## The Path of Care
For those who feel called to small, human acts.

This path includes:
- offering meals  
- supporting someone quietly  
- noticing hunger in daily life  
- practicing dignity in every interaction  

Care is the heartbeat of the movement.

## The Path of Community
For those who want to gather people.

This path includes:
- starting a Community Circle  
- hosting shared meals  
- creating spaces of belonging  
- nurturing connection  

Community grows through presence.

## The Path of Learning
For those who seek understanding.

This path includes:
- studying food systems  
- exploring dignity and solidarity  
- learning from cultures and communities  
- sharing knowledge with humility  

Learning deepens empathy.

## The Path of Creation
For those who express through making.

This path includes:
- writing  
- cooking  
- designing  
- documenting  
- building tools or resources  

Creation gives the movement form.

## The Path of Stewardship
For those who care for the movement itself.

This path includes:
- tending to culture  
- upholding principles  
- supporting Circles  
- keeping the movement grounded  

Stewardship protects the soul of the movement.

## The Path of Advocacy
For those who feel called to speak.

This path includes:
- sharing the movement with others  
- teaching  
- presenting  
- inviting people into the work  

Advocacy spreads the message with dignity.

## Walking More Than One Path
These pathways are not exclusive.  
Most people walk more than one.  
Some shift over time.  
Some walk quietly.  
Some walk boldly.

All paths are equal.  
All paths matter.

## Begin Where You Are
There is no starting point.  
There is only the next step.

Choose the path that feels alive for you —  
and let the movement grow through your presence.
