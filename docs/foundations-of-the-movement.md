# Foundations of the Movement

Open Food Systems is built on a set of foundational ideas that shape how we understand the world and how we work toward a future without hunger. These foundations are not rules — they are guiding truths that help us stay aligned as a global movement.

## 1. Humanity is One
We are many cultures, many histories, many experiences — but one human family.  
Hunger anywhere is a concern for all of us.

## 2. Dignity is Universal
Every person deserves access to nutritious food, not as charity, but as a basic expression of human dignity.

## 3. Knowledge Belongs to Everyone
When knowledge is open, humanity grows stronger.  
When it is locked away, we all become weaker.

## 4. Technology Should Serve Humanity
AI, data, and digital tools are not ends in themselves.  
They are instruments that must uplift people, not replace or diminish them.

## 5. Communities Know Themselves Best
Real solutions come from the lived experience of people and communities — not from distant institutions.

## 6. Collaboration is More Powerful Than Competition
We achieve more when we share ideas, tools, and insights openly.  
Collective intelligence is humanity’s greatest strength.

## 7. Transparency Builds Trust
We work in the open because trust grows when people can see, understand, and participate in the process.

## 8. Hope is a Form of Action
Believing in a better world is not naïve — it is the first step toward building one.

These foundations guide our movement as it grows, evolves, and adapts.  
They remind us who we are, what we stand for, and why we choose to build this future together.
