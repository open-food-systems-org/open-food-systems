# How to Contribute

Open Food Systems is a movement built by all of us.  
There is no single path to participation — only the desire to help create a world without hunger.

## Contribute Ideas
Share insights, questions, proposals, or lived experiences.  
Every idea helps us understand food systems more deeply.

## Contribute Knowledge
Add research, data sources, case studies, or community stories.  
Knowledge becomes more powerful when it is shared openly.

## Contribute Tools
Build or improve open-source tools that help communities understand, map, or strengthen their food systems.

## Support Local Efforts
Engage with food initiatives in your own community — gardens, cooperatives, food banks, farmers, educators, and local leaders.

## Connect People
Introduce others to the movement.  
Share resources, start conversations, and help build networks of collaboration.

## Learn and Grow
Read, explore, ask questions, and deepen your understanding of global and local food systems.

## Start Small
You don’t need expertise.  
You don’t need permission.  
You simply need to care — and to act.

Every contribution, no matter how small, strengthens the movement.
