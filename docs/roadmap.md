# Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the early direction of the Open Food Systems initiative. It is intentionally simple and will evolve as the project grows.

## Phase 1 — Foundation
- Establish the public website  
- Define the mission and movement  
- Create core documentation  
- Begin community outreach  

## Phase 2 — Exploration
- Map global food system challenges  
- Identify open-source opportunities  
- Explore AI-assisted food logistics  
- Study community-led food sovereignty models  

## Phase 3 — Prototyping
- Develop small, open-source tools  
- Test ideas with real communities  
- Document learnings openly  

## Phase 4 — Collaboration
- Invite contributors  
- Partner with aligned organizations  
- Support local food initiatives  

## Phase 5 — Scaling
- Expand tools, knowledge, and community  
- Strengthen global collaboration  
- Continue iterating toward a world without hunger  

This roadmap is a living document. It will change as the movement grows.
