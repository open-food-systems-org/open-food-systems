# Movement Symbols

Open Food Systems is a movement of people, not brands.  
Its symbols are simple, human, and universal —  
meant to be shared, drawn, carried, or remembered.

They are not logos.  
They are reminders.

## The Core Symbol: The Open Circle
A single open circle — a circle with a small gap.

It represents:
- a table with space for one more  
- a community that is never closed  
- a movement that welcomes without conditions  
- the idea that care flows in and out freely  

Anyone can draw it.  
Anyone can carry it.  
Anyone can make it their own.

## The Meaning of the Gap
The small opening in the circle symbolizes:
- humility  
- openness  
- the invitation to join  
- the belief that no community is complete  
- the space where care enters  

The gap is where dignity lives.

## The Colors of the Movement
The palette is warm, grounded, and human:

- **Earth Brown** — for soil, labor, and the hands that grow food  
- **Warm Orange** — for shared meals and nourishment  
- **Soft Green** — for growth, renewal, and community  
- **Gentle Cream** — for simplicity and dignity  

These colors are not rules.  
They are tones that reflect the spirit of the movement.

## The Gesture of the Movement
A simple gesture:  
**placing a hand lightly over the heart before sharing food.**

It expresses:
- gratitude  
- humility  
- presence  
- respect for the person receiving  
- acknowledgment of shared humanity  

This gesture can open a meal, a Circle, or a moment of care.

## Shared Language
A few short phrases that carry the movement’s spirit:

- **“There is always room at the table.”**  
- **“Care is shared.”**  
- **“We begin with dignity.”**  
- **“Food connects us.”**  

These phrases can open gatherings, close Circles, or simply remind us why we act.

## Symbols That Belong to Everyone
These symbols are not owned.  
They are not trademarked.  
They are not restricted.

They belong to anyone who believes in:
- dignity  
- care  
- community  
- shared humanity  

A movement grows when its symbols are carried by many.
